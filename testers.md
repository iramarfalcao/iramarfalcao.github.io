# Public Beta Program

Try Falcao SL apps before official release. As a beta tester, you get early access to upcoming features, test pre-release builds, and directly influence product design and performance.

---

## How to join

Participation is **free** and open to everyone:

## How to send feedback & report bugs

- **In TestFlight**: Take a screenshot while using the app and tap *Share Beta Feedback*, or submit feedback from the TestFlight dashboard.
- **In Google Play**: Leave private developer feedback directly on the app's store listing.
- **Via Email**: Reach out to [dev@falcaosl.com](mailto:dev@falcaosl.com). We read and personally reply to every report.
