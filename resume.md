# Resume

## Iramar Falcao

Software engineer who takes products end to end: design, code, ship, and keep
them running. Founder of [Falcao SL](https://falcaosl.com), a software studio
with three apps in production and seven in development.

<section class="resume-section">
  <h3>Profile</h3>
  <p>I build native applications for macOS, iOS, iPadOS and Android — SwiftUI where the platform deserves it, Kotlin Multiplatform where a shared core pays off — plus the Rust services behind them and the infrastructure that deploys everything. I have taken apps through the full App Store and Google Play path: signing, notarization, listings, privacy declarations, rejections and resubmissions.</p>
  <p>Recurring theme in my work: software that shows what it is going to do before doing it, and that keeps on the device whatever can stay on the device.</p>
</section>

<section class="resume-section">
  <h3>Selected work</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>S-Clean</strong> — macOS cleaner and uninstaller in SwiftUI, with widgets, a menu bar item, a 98-rule cleanup catalogue, 15 localizations, and zero network access. Went through signing, notarization and App Store review.</li>
    <li><strong>HyperEnv</strong> — open source (MIT) macOS environment manager, released on GitHub with CI, signed builds and a Homebrew Cask.</li>
    <li><strong>BR Generator</strong> — Flutter app for Android, iOS and web with twenty document generators and eleven validators, including the new alphanumeric CNPJ format; batch export to PDF, CSV and Excel.</li>
    <li><strong>Lira</strong> — Kotlin Multiplatform language app with an adapted SM-2 engine and a pluggable AI layer, backed by two Rust/actix services: a metered proxy with credits and spend caps, and a multi-language lexicon.</li>
    <li><strong>MeuPosto</strong> — Rust/actix + PostgreSQL/PostGIS back-end with geospatial search, OTP auth with rotating refresh tokens, a four-layer anti-fraud pipeline, OCR parsing and Bayesian ratings; KMP app on top.</li>
    <li><strong>Hortfrut</strong> — Rust/actix + HTMX management system with immutable price history, unit conversion and role scoping enforced in SQL.</li>
    <li><strong>Homelab</strong> — Proxmox, Cloudflare Tunnel, Coolify, Appwrite, MinIO and PostgreSQL, all declared in Terraform and Ansible; every product site deploys on push to main.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="resume-section">
  <h3>How I work</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Documentation lives next to the code — every product repository carries its product definition, status, monetization, distribution and marketing docs.</li>
    <li>Infrastructure is declared, never clicked.</li>
    <li>Privacy by default: no analytics or accounts unless the feature genuinely requires them.</li>
    <li>Nothing is promised on a site or a store listing before it exists in the build.</li>
  </ul>
